# Voice and tone

- Use concise product language and familiar Windows terms.
- Lead with current state, then the next useful action.
- Do not expose internal protocol names, Bluetooth identifiers or implementation jargon in ordinary UI.
- State unavailable hardware honestly: “尚未检测到” or “需要遥控器后验证”; never imply success from a running process alone.
- Buttons use direct verbs: “重新检测”, “打开日志目录”, “保存并启动”.
- Avoid exclamation marks and emoji. Use Chinese full-width punctuation in sentences.
