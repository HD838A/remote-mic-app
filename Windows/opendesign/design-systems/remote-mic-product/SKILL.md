# Remote Mic product UI

Use `tokens/colors_and_type.css` as the visual source of truth. Keep a 112–124 px navigation rail, a large page title, semantic blue selection, low-contrast cards, and at least 12 pt Chinese text. Keep device status and its recovery action in one card. Use the shared RC003 product photo without redrawing it.

Do not reproduce macOS window controls, platform-only permission names, BlackHole workflows, or unavailable product areas. Prefer Windows-native controls and settings links. Avoid decorative gradients, dense top tabs, long mixed-category dropdowns, and status colors used without real state meaning.
